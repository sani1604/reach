## FAQ (used on the landing page)

1. **Does OpenAI actually have ads?** Yes — OpenAI Ads lets brands reach users on ChatGPT. Reach plugs your Shopify store into that traffic so you can see what those clicks actually buy.
2. **Will this slow down my store?** No. The pixel loads asynchronously, and server-side events are sent from our servers in the background — never from your customer's browser.
3. **Do I need to edit my theme code?** No. Reach uses Shopify's Customer Events system to install the pixel automatically. No Liquid, no theme changes.
4. **Is my store data safe?** We forward only the events and order data needed for attribution, over HTTPS, and store your credentials encrypted.
5. **What happens after 50,000 events?** Your pixel keeps firing and your dashboard keeps working. Upgrade to Basic for unlimited scale plus revenue reporting and alerts.
